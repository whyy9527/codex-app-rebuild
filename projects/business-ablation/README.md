# Business ablation preparation

No formal business group has run. The live-host feasibility runs in projects/model-pipeline are separate engineering evidence and cannot be reused as the full-flow arm. Historical mechanism checks also remain separate.

The prospective comparison is full flow, without persisted behavior progress, without automatic Stop continuation, and without supplemental exploration. Each contrast must remove one consumer-visible mechanism while retaining valid frozen verification and equal maximum budgets. Disabling progress must not destroy the internal proof needed to run the verifier. Disabling Stop must allow normal within-turn model work; forcing every worker to stop after one attempt would bias that comparison.

All groups will start from the same historical choice-only Swift baseline, receive the same original two-lesson fixture and product brief, and receive the first four public acceptance cases. Supplemental exploration can reveal the other eight already observed public cases to the three discovery-enabled groups. This is structured replay of observations, not evidence of autonomous novel discovery. The no-discovery group can still implement the requested product features from the common brief.

[Holdout observation plan](HOLDOUT-PLAN.md) fixes three additional combinations before reference collection. Final budgets, repetitions, source manifests, group order and executable controller are not registered yet. Formal model execution must wait for that final registration to be committed and pushed. Preserve every failed group, unused budget, human intervention, time and obtainable usage; a small fixed sample does not establish statistical benefit.
