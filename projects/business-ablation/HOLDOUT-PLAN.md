# Holdout observation plan · 01

Register these three combined paths before collecting their original-UI observations. This is reference-data preparation, not a model experiment. All three cases must remain in the report, including unavailable or surprising results. Expected states are intentionally absent until observation. Record actual command outputs, input hashes, timestamps and reset receipts; do not infer server rules from Swift.

1. Correct choice → submit → quit → cancel quit → continue. Inspect feedback preservation, next-question selection and heart count.
2. Correct choice → change to wrong choice → submit → quit → cancel quit. Inspect final-selection judgment and preservation of incorrect feedback and hearts.
3. Seek word tiles → fill correct answer → remove answer token0 → remove answer token1. Inspect the one-token and empty-selection submission boundary. Indices identify words in the mapped correct answer, not their changing position in selected controls; both existing UI adapters use that meaning.

Use the actual original-adapter-v2 with a fresh ephemeral guest browser per scenario, original Spanish first lessons and the shared fixture-v2 initial-state contract. No authenticated account, subscription or backend reset. Preserve startup/reset/scenario/reset/stop outputs separately. A failure is an observation limit, not permission to replace a case with an easier one.

After collection, derive and freeze a separate three-case acceptance batch from the observed states. Before formal model groups, commit and push the final protocol, immutable original receipts, expected states, baseline product commit and input manifests. Keep these three cases and their outputs outside every implementation worker's visible inputs, including the full-flow arm. Do not use their scores to select a strategy or repair a worker. Seal product sources before scoring them once. Audit worker tool reads and report the procedural limits of filesystem separation.

The existing twelve cases are public acceptance scenarios and may be revealed during supplemental exploration. Their final scores cannot be presented as hidden generalization. These three combinations are the prospective held-out evaluation; if preparation or model access invalidates that separation, explicitly report leakage and do not label the affected scores hidden.
